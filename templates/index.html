Pharmly is built and maintained by Himanshu Sharma, a first-generation pharmacy graduate currently navigating the same fresher job hunt as thousands of other B.Pharm, M.Pharm, Pharm.D, and D.Pharm graduates across India.

Himanshu built Pharmly out of a very real, personal frustration: countless job listings labeled "fresher" that actually demanded 1-3 years of experience, with no real way to filter out the noise.

Our Core Mission & Attribution

Pharmly aggregates listings from platforms like PharmaBharat.com and PharmaRecruiter.com — with full credit and a direct link back to the original posting for every single job.

It tags each job by genuine degree eligibility (B.Pharm / M.Pharm / Pharm.D / D.Pharm), so freshers spend less time scrolling and more time applying to real opportunities.

Legal Notice & Fair Use

Non-Commercial Project • Last Updated: July 2026
Pharmly is a free, non-commercial, personal community project built solely for educational & job discovery assistance.

1. Purpose & Non-Commercial Nature

Pharmly is a free, non-commercial personal project built by a B.Pharm graduate to help pharmacy freshers across India find genuine entry-level job openings. It is NOT distributed on commercial app stores, contains NO ads, subscriptions, paid features, or data resale.

2. Data Sourcing & Credit

Pharmly aggregates publicly available job listing data from PharmaBharat.com and PharmaRecruiter.com. All scraping is rate-limited to minimize load. Pharmly does not claim ownership over any job content — all credit belongs to original source sites and employers. Users are always provided direct links to original postings.

3. Fair Use Position

Operated under good-faith, non-commercial intent for aiding job discovery for an underserved audience (pharmacy freshers), consistent with general fair-use/fair-dealing principles.

4. Takedown & Direct Contact Policy
