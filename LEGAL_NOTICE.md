# Legal Notice & Fair Use Disclaimer — Pharmly

**Last updated:** July 2026

## Purpose & Nature of This Project

Pharmly is a free, non-commercial, personal project built by a B.Pharm graduate to help pharmacy freshers across India find genuine entry-level job openings. It is:

- **Not** distributed on the Google Play Store or any app store
- **Not** monetized in any way — no ads, no subscriptions, no paid features, no data resale, no lead-selling
- Built and maintained purely for educational purposes and personal/community job-search assistance

## Data Sourcing

Pharmly aggregates publicly available job listing data from **PharmaBharat.com** and **PharmaRecruiter.com**. All scraping is rate-limited and designed to minimize load on source servers. Pharmly does not claim ownership over any job listing content — all credit for original postings belongs to PharmaBharat.com, PharmaRecruiter.com, and the employers who posted them. Users are always encouraged to visit the original source page to view full details and apply.

## Fair Use Position

This project is operated under a good-faith, non-commercial intent for the purpose of aiding job discovery for an underserved audience (pharmacy freshers), consistent with general fair-use/fair-dealing principles. This statement reflects the maintainer's intent and understanding — it is not a legal determination, and fair use is ultimately something courts evaluate case by case, not something a maintainer can unilaterally establish.
